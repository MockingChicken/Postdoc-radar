# Postdoc Radar

Open postdoc and scientist positions in cancer, molecular, cell and applied biology at universities, institutes, hospitals and companies in the UK, Europe and the US.

**Open the app:** https://mockingchicken.github.io/Postdoc-radar/

- Search and filter by region, city, position, employer type, field and topic (cell cycle, DNA repair, chromatin and more).
- Track your applications: stages, a tick-box checklist, applied and interview dates, notes and history. Your tracker is saved in your own browser and is never uploaded; use **Back up** / **Restore from backup** to move it between devices.
- The posting list (`jobs.json`) is refreshed every morning by an automated search of job boards, institute and company career pages and lab websites.

Always confirm details on the original advert before applying.

## Files

- `index.html` – the app (a single self-contained page).
- `jobs.json` – the live posting list the app loads when it opens.
- `tools/build_feed.py` – builds `jobs.json` from the exported posting list.
